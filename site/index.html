    How to play
    • Everyone gets 5 cards. On your turn, draw from the deck or take the top discard, then discard one card. You have 30 seconds.
    • Spreads: three or four of a kind, or 3+ cards in a row of the same suit (Ace is low: A‑2‑3). Laying them down is optional.
    • You can add cards to anyone's spreads on the table.
    • Card points: Ace 1, number cards face value, J/Q/K 10.
    • Empty your hand to win the round — or hit DROP if you think you hold the lowest points. Get caught (someone ties or beats you) and you take a 10‑point penalty.
    • Dealt exactly 21? DROP on your first turn before drawing and win on the spot. Nobody will tell you — count your own cards.
    • Everyone except the round winner adds the points left in their hand to their score. First to win 3 rounds wins the game.
